Acme Corp IT Security Protocol
Last Updated: May 2026

Password Requirements
All employee passwords must be a minimum of 14 characters, containing at least one uppercase letter, one number, and one symbol. Passwords automatically expire and must be rotated every 90 days.

Remote Access
Employees accessing the Acme Corp intranet from a remote location must use the approved company VPN client (Cisco AnyConnect). Split-tunneling is strictly disabled for security reasons.

Incident Reporting
If you suspect your laptop has been compromised, or if you receive a highly suspicious phishing email, you must immediately forward the details to the Security Operations team at sec-ops@acmecorp.com. Do NOT forward the email to your manager.
